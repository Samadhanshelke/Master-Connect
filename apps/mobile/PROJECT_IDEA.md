# Master Connect - Project Idea & Documentation

## Overview
**Master Connect** is a hyper-local community platform designed to connect people within their specific Master community. It bridges the gap between social networking and local commerce, fostering a tight-knit community environment.

## Core Features

### 1. Master-Based Community Groups
*   **Auto-Join/Select**: Users join a group specific to their Master community.
*   **Real-time Chat**: A central hub for general discussions, announcements, and community bonding.
*   **Sub-groups (Optional)**: Potential for interest-based channels within the Community (e.g., "Sports", "News", "Help").

### 2. Social Feed & Posts
*   **User Posts**: Users can share updates, news, photos, or questions.
*   **Visibility Control**:
    *   **Global**: Visible to everyone on the platform.
    *   **Community-Only**: Visible only to members of the user's specific community.
*   **Interactions**: Likes, comments, and shares to foster engagement.

### 3. Local Vendor Marketplace (The "Shop")
*   **Vendor Profiles**: Local shop owners and service providers can create business profiles.
*   **Product Listings**: Vendors can showcase what they sell (images, prices, descriptions).
*   **Local Discovery**: Users can browse shops specifically within their area, supporting local businesses.
*   **Direct Inquiry**: Chat feature to contact vendors directly for orders or queries.

## User Roles
1.  **Citizen (User)**: Joins Community, chats, posts, browses shops.
2.  **Vendor**: All Citizen features + ability to create a Shop and list products.
3.  **Admin/Moderator**: Manages reported content and ensures community guidelines are followed.

## Design Philosophy
*   **Theme**: "Local & Vibrant".
*   **Light Mode**: Clean, airy, and approachable. Uses whitespace effectively to avoid clutter.
*   **Dark Mode**: Sleek, high-contrast, and comfortable for low-light usage.
*   **Color Palette**:
    *   **Primary (Teal/Green)**: Represents growth, trust, and the "local" ground-level connection.
    *   **Accent (Amber/Orange)**: Highlights commerce, shops, and important actions.

## Future Scope
*   **Events**: Local event calendars.
*   **Emergency Services**: Quick access to local police, hospitals, and fire stations.
*   **Classifieds**: Buy/Sell used goods within the community.
