# Master Connect - App Key Features

## 1. Community Feed (Home)
The central hub for community interaction, allowing users to share updates and engage with others.
- **Rich Post Content**: Users can create posts containing:
  - Text with **@mentions** and **URL highlighting**.
  - **Images** (single or gallery view with pagination).
  - **Interactive Polls** with real-time voting visuals.
  - **Documents** (PDFs, etc.) as attachments.
- **Social Interactions**:
  - **Like, Comment, & Bookmark** posts.
  - **Follow** users to see their content.
  - **Share** posts with others.
- **Post Management (Three-dot Menu)**:
  - **Share Post**: Share content with others.
  - **Report Post**: Flag inappropriate content for moderation.
  - **Delete Post**: Remove your own posts (only visible on your posts).
- **Create Post Flow**:
  - Dedicated modal for creating posts.
  - Visibility settings (**Local** vs **Global** audience).
  - Quick attachment tools for images, polls, and documents.

## 2. Community Chat & Leaders
A comprehensive communication platform for connecting with city groups and community leaders.

### City Groups
- **Join City Groups**: Users can request to join city-based community groups.
- **City Chat Rooms**: Real-time group chat for local community discussions.
- **Member Count**: See how many locals are connected in each group.

### Community Leaders
- **Leader Directory**: View community leaders organized by month (last 5 months).
- **Voting System**: 
  - Vote for your preferred community leader each month.
  - **One vote per user per month** - ensures fair voting.
  - **City-specific voting** - vote only for leaders in your city.
  - Real-time vote percentage display with visual progress bars.
  - Voted cards get highlighted with distinct styling.
- **Add Leader Nominations**:
  - Submit nominations for new community leaders.
  - Include photo, name, and designation.
  - Requests are tracked and reviewed.
- **Leader Chat**: Direct messaging with individual leaders.

### Chat Room Features
- **Live Messaging**: Interactive real-time chat interface.
- **Rich Message Types**:
  - Send **Text** messages with @mentions and URL highlighting.
  - Share **Images** with preview.
  - Upload **Documents** (PDFs, spreadsheets, etc.).
  - **Create & Share Polls** for instant community feedback.
- **Poll Interactions**: Vote on polls and see real-time results.
- **User Presence**: See who is active and identify messages easily.
- **Message Management (Long-press)**:
  - **Delete Message**: Remove your own messages from the chat.
  - **Report Message**: Flag inappropriate messages from other users.

### Request Management
- **Request History Screen**: View all past requests (join group, add leader).
- **Status Tracking**: See request status (pending, approved, rejected).
- **Request Details**: View submission date and request specifics.

## 6. Banner Advertising
Paid promotional banners displayed on the Home screen.

### Banner Rules
- **One Day Visibility**: Each banner is displayed for exactly one day.
- **One Banner Per Day**: Only one banner can be shown on any given day.
- **No Changes After Payment**: Banner image cannot be modified once payment is complete.
- **Available Dates Only**: Users can only select dates that are not already booked.

### Booking Flow
1. **Select Date**: Choose an available date from the calendar.
2. **Upload Banner**: Upload a banner image (1200×400 recommended).
3. **Submit Request**: Send request for admin approval.
4. **Await Approval**: Admin reviews the banner content.
5. **Make Payment**: Once approved, complete payment via payment gateway.
6. **Banner Goes Live**: Banner is displayed on the selected date.

### Features
- **Calendar View**: Visual calendar showing booked and available dates.
- **Request Tracking**: View status of all banner requests (pending, approved, rejected).
- **Payment Integration**: Secure payment gateway for banner fees.
- **Preview**: See how the banner will look before submitting.

### Access
- Navigate to **Profile → Settings → Services → Banner Advertising**

## 7. User Roles & Use Cases

### Regular User
The default role for all community members.
- **Feed**: Create posts, like, comment, bookmark, and share content.
- **Chat**: Join city groups, participate in discussions, create polls.
- **Shops**: Browse local businesses, view shop details.
- **Jobs**: Browse job listings, contact employers.
- **Profile**: Manage personal information, view own posts and saved items.
- **Leaders**: Vote for community leaders, nominate new leaders.

### Shop Owner
Users who have registered their business on the platform.
- All **Regular User** capabilities, plus:
- **Create Shop**: List business with details (name, category, contact, location).
- **Manage Shop**: Edit shop information, update business hours.
- **Post Jobs**: Create job listings for their business.
- **Manage Jobs**: Edit or close job postings, view applicant inquiries.

### Community Leader
Recognized leaders who actively contribute to the community.
- All **Regular User** capabilities, plus:
- **Direct Messaging**: Receive messages from community members seeking guidance.
- **Visibility**: Featured in the Leaders directory with vote counts.
- **Monthly Recognition**: Leaders are organized and displayed by month.

### Admin (Future)
Platform administrators with moderation capabilities.
- **Content Moderation**: Review reported posts and messages.
- **User Management**: Handle user-related issues and violations.
- **Community Oversight**: Approve/reject group join requests and leader nominations.
- **Analytics**: Access to platform usage statistics.
